- 2026-11-04 (R4 T2): "drawn vs the owner's report now" (engine: vehicle position (R3 T5)) is self-referential: the ideal is the owner's snapshots extrapolated by the SAME interpolator to NearT + the SAME link lag the aim uses, so a wrong link lag or a dead-reckoning error moves the copy and the reference together and the live number stays small (simulated: a 30 ms lag error is 0.4 m of real error at 15 m/s and 0.00 m of metric). - 2026-11-04 (R4 T2): a proportional position correction on top of a STALE velocity feed-forward settles at e = (feed-forward error) / gain, not at zero: the near-car clock draws a copy 100-200 ms past its newest snapshot with that snapshot's velocity, so a braking car (a = 9 m/s^2) leaves a steady offset of a*L/g = 0.5-0.65 m for as long as it brakes, which is exactly the stretch after a crash. The sim's 'persistent offset after a rear-end that scales with RTT but not with jitter, loss, grace, adoption or the arbiter' was the signature; look for such a steady state in the phase the target is moving non-uniformly before blaming the contact logic.
