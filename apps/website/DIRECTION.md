# solar-rp.com: direction

Owner brief (2026-11-02): "it needs to be cool, gta5 themed, and wicked animations, gsap, lenis, three.js anything." Rejected: a calm install-sheet page, a loading-screen page, a timestamped walkthrough. Discord: not yet (the page says "Discord soon", no link).

Fixed by the project: NOON palette and the Solar RP brand kit (`brand/solar/solar-rp-noon`), real GTA V footage and key art (the launcher's loops, the three official loading-screen splashes), no Rockstar/GTA V logos, no Pricedown, never implying an official Rockstar product. Home-made 3D GTA scenes are banned (rejected twice); the only real-time 3D is our own S mark, extruded from the brand vector, and WebGL treatment of real footage.

## Bar (reference sites)

1. **rockstargames.com/VI** (GTA VI trailer site). Full-bleed official key art per character, enormous condensed titles, scroll-driven image reveals that scale a still from a framed panel to full screen, sunset/teal palettes taken from the art itself. What to take: the art carries everything, type is huge and few words, every scroll beat is one image doing one move.
2. **playvalorant.com** (Riot). Hard-edged type, one rationed accent colour, cut corners, video in the hero, sections that snap between dense and quiet. What to take: a single accent used like a weapon, angular geometry as the brand's grammar.
3. **apple.com product pages** (AirPods Pro / iPhone launches). Pinned scroll sequences where one object turns and text arrives exactly in time with it. What to take: the object animation and the copy share one scroll clock; one object, many shots.

## Three directions

### A. COVER GRID
The GTA V box art is a grid of hard-edged, tilted panels, each a character or a moment. The page builds that grid from our key art and footage: Michael, Franklin and Trevor splashes plus the loops, with the gold S mark owning one cell the way the logo owns the box's corner cell. Scroll explodes the grid and flies each panel to full screen as its section. Hero frame: six hard-cut panels on ground #0a0907, Michael largest left, the S cell gold, SOLAR RP set across the bottom row. Risk: reads as a GTA V cover parody if the panels copy the box layout too literally.

### B. SUNDOWN COLD OPEN
A trailer cold open. Letterboxed 2.39:1 footage of Los Santos in golden haze; the 3D S, ink and gold, stands large in the frame catching the sun (environment reflections from the footage itself). Scroll opens the letterbox to full bleed, turns the S edge-on and sends it into the sun, then cuts through the loops with a gold burn transition while single-line title cards punch in. Hero frame: black bars top and bottom, city footage, the S centred-left at 55% of the frame height, the lockup and DOWNLOAD below it. Risk: a single 3D logo can look like a screensaver if its material is weak.

### C. WANTED LEVEL
GTA's HUD language: the radar, the wanted stars, the mission-passed card. The page is a "mission": DOWNLOAD is the objective, the scroll fills the stars. Hero frame: night footage, a five-star meter across the top, MISSION: GET IN. Risk: pastiche of Rockstar's own HUD, closest to imitating an official product; cheapest-looking of the three.

## Tournament

The three hero silhouettes are in `direction/hero-a.svg`, `hero-b.svg`, `hero-c.svg` (PNG beside each).

**Verdict (visual-critic, 2026-11-02): B wins, with one beat from A.** B reads as "GTA trailer" instantly without copying Rockstar's own layouts; one subject and one move per beat. A copies the box-art layout (implies an official product), shrinks the S to a cell and turns to mush on phones. C is a template stack dressed as Rockstar's HUD. Dropped.
- Taken from A: only the character beat. Michael, Franklin and Trevor as hard-cut panels growing from framed to full screen one at a time, cut at the mark's 45-degree angle, not the box tilt.
- Biggest risk of B: the S as a pasted-on screensaver logo, and the gold hook lost in golden haze. Beaten by: two materials (gold metal, roughness 0.3; ink satin with clearcoat), environment lit from the same footage, a rim light at the footage's sun, a visible chamfer highlight, at most 15 degrees of turn in the hero (three-quarter views read as a "5"), no idle spin, placed against dark building mass, and the same grain and haze over the S and the footage.
- Transitions: a 45-degree gold slash wipe taken from the mark's cut (not a stock "burn").
- Type: title cards are one condensed line at 12-16vw; the lockup stays small.
- Phones: a 4:5 crop instead of the 2.39:1 letterbox.

## Subject read test

The hero subject is the Solar RP S: two hooks forming an S on a 1:2 grid. Its silhouette reads as an "S" in under 2 s at any size (it is the letterform). Extruded 18% of its width deep with a 45-degree chamfer, the upper hook in warm ink metal, the lower hook in gold.
