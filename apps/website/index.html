What SOL runs today

The Solar RP client runs inside your GTA V and talks to SOL over our own protocol. This is what it does now.

  • The streets are players

    No ambient traffic and no pedestrians: the people and cars you pass are other players.

  • A HUD made for roleplay

    A vitals ring with the map inside it, and a car cluster with speed, fuel, blinkers, lights, lock and seatbelt.

  • Cars look the same on every screen

    Lights, sirens, steering, tyres, windows and damage are shared between players.

  • The server has the last word

    SOL keeps the world state and checks what every client sends.

How to join

You need GTA V for PC. The launcher does the rest.

  1. 1

    Download Solar Launcher

    Install it on Windows 10 or 11. It finds your GTA V by itself, or you point it at the folder once.

  2. 2

    Pick SOL

    The launcher lists our servers and shows which are up. Choose SOL.

  3. 3

    Press PLAY

    The launcher starts GTA V with the Solar RP client, joins the server, and tells you every step while it does.
