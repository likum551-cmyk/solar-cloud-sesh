How SOL plays

Cars look the same on every screen

The server checks what every client sends and passes each car's state to everyone near it.

  • Headlights, high beams, sirens and the horn
  • Steering, tyres, windows and brake lights
  • Body damage

The server keeps track of what you carry

The server checks every item you carry, and the client draws its own HUD: health and hunger around the map, and a speedometer with fuel and car status.

  • Pockets, a backpack and car trunks
  • Drop an item and anyone nearby can pick it up
  • The clothes you wear are items too

Questions

Do I need my own copy of GTA V?

Today, yes: GTA V for PC, Legacy or Enhanced, from Steam, Epic Games or the Rockstar Games Launcher. Support for cracked copies is coming soon.

Does it cost anything?

No. Solar Launcher and a Solar RP account are free.

Is this GTA Online?

No. Solar RP has its own servers, accounts and client. It is not made by or affiliated with Rockstar Games.

Can Legacy and Enhanced players play together?

Yes. Both editions join the same SOL server.

How does the launcher update?

It updates itself from solar-rp.com and checks the signature of every update before installing it. The SHA-256 of the current installer is listed under the download button below.

When do LUNA and TERRA open?

They are planned, with no date yet. They will show up in the launcher's server list when they open, and the account you make today works on them too.
